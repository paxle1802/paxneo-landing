WiFi Meter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: WiFi Meter collects no data. The app has no server, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything it measures stays on your Mac.

1. Wi-Fi information the app reads

The app asks macOS (through Apple's CoreWLAN) about your current Wi-Fi connection: signal strength, noise, link rate, channel, Wi-Fi standard, security type and network name. When you open the Nearby networks tab, it also asks macOS to scan for networks around you. This information is only shown to you.

The chart keeps only the last 5 minutes of readings in memory while the app runs and never writes them to disk. Scan results are not saved either. Quitting the app discards them.

The app stores only your settings on your Mac: language, weak-signal threshold, and whether alerts are on.

2. Location permission

On newer versions of macOS, Apple only reveals Wi-Fi network names to apps allowed to use Location Services, so the app may ask for that permission. The app never reads, stores or sends your location; the permission only lets macOS show network names. You can decline: the app still measures the signal but cannot show network names.

3. Speed test

When you press Start test, the app runs the networkQuality tool built into macOS. It sends and receives test data with Apple's servers to measure speed, so those servers see your IP address like any Internet connection, under Apple's privacy policy. The app sends nothing else and does not keep results after you quit.

4. Notifications

Weak-signal alerts are local notifications created on your Mac. The app does not use any server push service.

5. Third parties

The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and shares data with no one. Apple may provide developers with anonymous crash reports if you opted in to share with developers in your Mac's settings, under Apple's own policy.

6. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.

8. Developer

WiFi Meter is a free, independent app published on the Mac App Store by an individual developer (the name shown on the app's App Store page).

9. Contact
